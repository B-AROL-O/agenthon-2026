# AGENTHON 2026: Track 2 social media captions

<!-- (2026-11-21) -->

Draft captions for **deliverable 2**, the social media content set, in [WP2](https://github.com/B-AROL-O/agenthon-2026/issues/9).
They follow the copy system in the [concept lock](track2-concept-lock.md) and the tone of voice in the [brand and product kit](feyles-brand-and-product-kit.md).
Feyles is on Instagram ([@grissinificiofeyles1968](https://www.instagram.com/grissinificiofeyles1968/)) and TikTok ([@grissinificiofeyles](https://www.tiktok.com/@grissinificiofeyles)), so the set targets those two.

Captions are in Italian, the audience's language; an English gloss follows each one for the jury.

## Copy rules

- **Register:** plural «voi» for the audience, «noi» for the bakery. Short sentences, concrete nouns.
- **Never use:** eccellenza, passione, segreto di famiglia, unico, premium, gourmet, esperienza, autentico, artigianalità.
- **Never say** "fatto a mano" or "hand-rolled", and never claim awards, founders, volumes or anything the client kit does not state.
- **CTAs** only from the approved list: «Scoprite gli otto gusti.», «Ordinate online: spediamo in tutta Italia.», «Venite a trovarci in piazza Castello.», «Componete la vostra box regalo.»
- **No emoji in brand copy.** The brand's voice is plain and understated.
- **Campaign hashtag:** `#SiDivideSiCondivide`, always first. Then at most four more from `#Feyles #Rubatà #grissini #aperitivo #Torino`.
- **Pronunciation for voice and video:** Rubatà is ru-ba-TÀ, stress on the last syllable.

## Posts

### 1. Spot cut-down: Reel and TikTok (9:16)

Visual: the 26.5-second spot in 9:16, captions burned in for viewing with no sound.

> Uno diventa due.
> Il Rubatà si spezza con un crac, e metà è già di qualcun altro.
> Si divide. Si condivide.
>
> Scoprite gli otto gusti: link in bio.
>
> #SiDivideSiCondivide #Feyles #Rubatà #aperitivo

_English:_ One becomes two. A Rubatà breaks with a crack, and half of it already belongs to someone else. It breaks. It's shared. Discover the eight flavours: link in bio.

**TikTok variant**, shorter, inviting people to post their own snap:

> Fate sentire il vostro crac.
> Spezzate un Rubatà, passate l'altra metà, taggate chi l'ha presa.
>
> #SiDivideSiCondivide #Rubatà #aperitivo

_English:_ Let us hear your crack. Break a Rubatà, pass the other half, tag whoever took it.

### 3. `social-finestra-1x1`: feed post

Visual: macro on the die-cut wheat-ear window of the `Il Classico` carton, knobbly breadsticks behind it.

> Dentro c'è quello che deve esserci.
> Farina di un mulino di fiducia, strutto, malto, sale e lievito.
> L'impasto riposa, si arrotola, si sforna ogni giorno.
>
> Il Classico, da cui è cominciato tutto.
> Ordinate online: spediamo in tutta Italia.
>
> #SiDivideSiCondivide #Feyles #Rubatà #grissini

_English:_ Inside is what should be inside. Flour from a trusted mill, lard, malt, salt and yeast. The dough rests, is rolled, and is baked every day. Il Classico, where it all began. Order online: we ship across Italy.

Note: the concept lock mentions "four base ingredients", but `Il Classico` also contains lard (`strutto`), so the caption lists five. Lard is the only ingredient that sets this flavour apart, and leaving it out would misstate the product.
