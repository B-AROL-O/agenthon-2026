## Gifting economics and conversion thresholds

Six verified price and tax thresholds shape how Feyles converts both private party hosts (25–40 years old) and local small-business gift buyers:

- **€19.80 host-gift combo (illustrative):** 1 × `Cioccorubatà al Gianduia` (€6.50) + 1 × `Granin Gentile` 280 g (€6.00) + 1 × `Rubatà Nocciole IGP` 200 g (€3.40) + 1 × `Brichet Cacio e Pepe` 200 g (€3.90) — under €20 to replace the cliché bottle of wine at dinner parties.
- **€39.00 free shipping threshold:** Replaces the standard €9.99 national GLS courier fee; orders placed before 12:00 on weekdays dispatch same day (1–2 working days).
- **€41.20 live tasting bundle:** The existing 12-pack assortment (all 8 Rubatà + all 4 Brichet, 2.4 kg total) clears free GLS shipping automatically.
- **€48.00 national benchmark:** Cited from Coldiretti/Ixè Christmas 2025 data for average Italian household spend on a food gift basket (with 34% of families gifting 9.7–10.2 million food baskets annually and 5 million buying in the final week).
- **€50.00 Italian B2B cap:** Statutory ceiling for 100% tax-deductible and VAT-deductible corporate client gifts (illustrative €49.00 mixed savoury-sweet assortment sits just below this cap, paired with in-cart SDI/PEC invoicing).
- **€65.00 (5% off) and €100.00 (10% off):** Automatic cart volume discounts (bulk 1.5–3 kg cartons ship on €35 pallets outside Turin province, free from €150, or at standard rates within Turin province).

## Rubatà craft and local Piedmontese ecosystem

Deep research highlights technical and civic facts that explain why the product looks and sounds distinct on camera:

- **Rubatà (PAT no. 309) vs. Stirato (PAT no. 273):** _Stirato_ dough is pulled lengthwise to about 1.5 m and bakes thin, straight, and uniform.
  _Rubatà_ ("rolled" in Piedmontese) is divided into rested dough portions (_filoncini_), rolled across a board dusted with fine bran flour (_cruschello_) into knobbly, irregular sticks under 40–50 cm, and baked in falling-temperature ovens (250 °C down to 200 °C) until dry and crisp.
- **Historical celebration context:** Historical accounts noted by Feyles describe 17th- and 18th-century _rubatà_ not as everyday bread, but as a festive delicacy baked for wealthy households.
- **Civic and culinary network:**
  - Grassroots solidarity buying groups (G.A.S. via PortAlGas across Piedmont and Valle d'Aosta) and the `Pistaaa` slow-mobility cycling food route across Cambiano, Chieri, and Baldissero.
  - Community ties with the Santena Pro-Loco, Friends of the Camillo Cavour Foundation, and Cambiano parish.
  - Culinary partnerships with Turin master chocolatier Davide Appendino (_Maestro del Gusto_), restaurants San Giors and D'Amblé (which coat their _Grissinopoli_ Fassone cutlet in Feyles Rubatà), and 2026 events including Salone del Vino Torino (>19,000 visitors), Terra Madre Salone del Gusto (Piazzetta Reale), Festival Car, and Savor Piemonte in Reykjavik.
  - Brand voice case study: after a break-in at the Cambiano facility in June 2025, Feyles posted on Instagram _"Lo capiamo: sono troppo buoni"_ ("We get it: they are too good"), capturing its understated Piedmontese humour.

## Early narrative explorations and pivot to the official brief

Before the official 10:28 Track 2 brief was released, initial deep research explored three Christmas-centred commercial concepts:

1. **17 km of aroma:** 7:00 morning oven mouth in Cambiano to dusk lights under the Piazza Castello 49 porticos, ending with a _CRACK_ on a shared board.
2. **The first gift opened before midnight:** A Feyles box brought to a holiday party that gets opened five minutes after the doorbell rings instead of waiting under the tree.
3. **Every knot is different:** Macro slow-motion contrast between irregular _rubatà_ and uniform industrial sticks.

When the official brief and client kit arrived, the team locked a sharper direction in [Track 2 concept lock](track2-concept-lock.md):

- **Seasonal framing dropped:** Christmas is not part of the brief, which targets 25–40 year-olds for year-round home aperitivo and convivial gifting.
- **Catalog scope narrowed:** Instead of rotating all 19 SKUs in 26 seconds, the spot and hero visuals focus on one gesture and one protagonist carton (`Il Classico` 200 g).
- **Insights kept:** The crisp acoustic snap opening the social moment, the irregular knobbly silhouette, Turin porticos proximity, and the gift-as-shared-gesture reframe.
