# AGENTHON 2026: Track 2 challenge brief

<!-- (2026-11-21) -->

Official brief for Track 2, revealed at 10:28 on 21 November 2026 on the [AGENTHON portal]((participant portal)).
Deliverables are due by 17:00 on the portal.

## The challenge

- Track: 2, GenAI for marketing
- Track sponsor: [Demetra](https://demetradesign.it/)

### The client

Feyles – Grissinificio is a Turin-based company that makes Rubatà, the hand-rolled breadsticks of Piedmont.

- Goal: make them the star of aperitivo at home and of time with friends, and a gift idea too.
- Target: 25 to 40 year-olds.

### The brief

Make the grissino the protagonist: turn its shape, its crunch and the act of sharing it into a story that surprises and engages.

Show its artisanal character in a contemporary language: the breadstick for the next aperitivo, and a gift that stands out when friends get together.

### The project

A 20 to 30 second commercial with visuals made with generative AI, plus social content and visuals for web and traditional ads.

AI voices, music, sound effects and editing are welcome.
One recognizable idea, one consistent look, one clear message.

Any style: Realistic · Illustrated · Animated · Surreal.

### Materials provided

- Brand logo
- Product photos
- Verified product info
- Tone of voice guide

Packaging and product must stay faithful to the originals, as far as possible.
Settings, characters, situations and effects are open to interpretation.

## Deliverables

Deliver by 17:00 on the portal.

1. Advertising spot, 20 to 30 seconds
2. Social Media Content Set, adaptable to the main channels
3. Web and print visuals in line with the concept
4. Advertising image with headline and CTA
5. Project sheet: idea, concept, tools and the role of AI in your work
6. Tools list: platforms and software used, and what they were used for

## How to win

### The jury

Each track has its own: 5 people, 1 from the track sponsor, 1 from GDG Torino and 3 external.

### Judged on

- Innovation
- Fit with the track theme
- Feasibility beyond the prototype
- Quality of the technical execution
- Quality of the pitch

### The pitch

About three minutes per team in Agentic AI for business and five in GenAI for marketing, in your track's room, in front of its jury, who can ask you questions.
Show the problem, the solution and the demo: a demo that works beats ten slides.

Only 8 to 12 teams per track pitch: the jury picks the finalists from all the submitted projects.

### Prizes

- Amazon gift cards for the top two teams in each track, split equally among the team members.
- Each jury also gives books, courtesy of Apogeo, O'Reilly and Packt, to projects that stand out, even off the podium.
- Special prize from tech partner ElevenLabs: Best use of ElevenLabs.
