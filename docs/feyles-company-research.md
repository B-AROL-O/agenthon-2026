## Watch out before writing copy

- **"Hand-rolled".**
  The brief says hand-rolled, and La Pecora Nera says "lavorati a mano".
  The official site never says "fatto a mano" or "hand-rolled", and one of its photos shows a conveyor belt of "centinaia di grissini" leaving the oven.
  Check the verified product info before using "hand-rolled" as a claim.
- **"Turin-based".**
  The bakery is in Cambiano, about 20 km south-east of Turin, and the registered office and one shop are in Turin.
  "From Turin" is fair. Saying the grissini are baked in the city centre is not.
- **Lard.**
  The Classico, the best-known flavour, is made with lard (strutto), so it is not vegetarian.
  Avoid "vegan" or "plant-based" for the whole range.
- **Gluten.**
  Every product contains gluten, and there is no gluten-free line.
- **Old names.**
  Older press uses "Sesamino", "Speciale" and Santena.
  The current names are "Tre Semi" and "Rubatà Speziati", and the bakery has been in Cambiano since early 2025.

## The rubatà

- **The name.**
  Piedmontese for "rolled". The rubatà is "nodoso, irregolare e friabile": knobbly, irregular and crisp, and no two are alike.
  Turismo Torino describes the move behind the name: the stretched dough is held by the tips and dropped onto the board in a full turn, "with gentle violence", to compact it.
- **Against the stirato.**
  The grissino stirato is stretched, thin and even. The rubatà is rolled, knobbly and irregular.
- **Status.**
  Both are on Piedmont's list of traditional agri-food products (PAT): rubatà is entry 309 and grissino stirato entry 273.
  Turismo Torino calls it "rubatà di Chieri".
- **Origin story.**
  Tradition says the grissino was created in Turin in 1679 by court baker Antonio Brunero for the young Duke Victor Amadeus II.
  The Feyles site itself calls this "handed down rather than documented".
  Another popular anecdote has Napoleon calling them "les petits bâtons de Turin".
- **How Feyles makes it.**
  The dough rests, then it is worked and baked: "Every stage takes the time it needs."
  Flour comes from "un mulino di cui ci fidiamo" (a mill they trust). The Savor Piemonte listing says non-GMO certified flours.
  The base recipe is soft wheat flour, malt, salt and brewer's yeast, plus lard or oil depending on the flavour.
  No length, oven or bake time is published.
- **How they suggest eating it.**
  Instead of bread, with cured meats and cheese, at aperitivo, or as a snack.
  A Turin restaurant, San Giors, used Feyles rubatà as the breading of its signature cutlet (La Stampa, May 2025).
- **Shelf life.**
  Up to 4 months loose and 6 months packed.

## Tone of voice on their site

Warm, plain and direct, never salesy. Short sentences.
The Italian addresses customers with the plural "voi": "Scriveteci, chiamateci", "Venite a trovarci!".
The message is continuity: the same family, the same bakery, the same grissino.

Phrases the site repeats:

- "Grissini Rubatà, dal 1968." / "Rubatà grissini, since 1968." This is the sign-off on every page.
- "Una famiglia, un laboratorio, lo stesso grissino da più di cinquant'anni."
- "Dentro c'è quello che deve esserci."
- "Si sforna ogni giorno." / "We bake every day."
- "Dietro ogni grissino ci siamo noi." / "We're behind every single grissino."
- "Il grissino è rimasto lo stesso." / "The grissino has stayed the same."
- "Il grissino friabile e fragrante, la specialità di Feyles."
- "dal forno a casa vostra"

The site describes no brand colours or fonts; take them from the logo and packaging in the official materials.
La Cucina Italiana describes the packs in the Turin shop as colourful.
Their own photos include product boxes, a linen bag on a set table, and archive shots of the old Santena bakery, including a 1987 family film.

## What this suggests for our campaign

- **They already make sharing and gifting products.**
  The 12-flavour bundle, the Christmas box and the 750 g packs give the gift message real products to show.
- **They sit by Palazzo Reale.**
  The shop stands in Piazza Castello, where the 1679 court story is set.
- **The irregular shape fits the brief.**
  "No two are alike" turns the knobbly look into the selling point.
- **Their channels fit the 25 to 40 audience.**
  They are on Instagram and TikTok, so the social set can be built for those two.
- **The house tone is quiet.**
  Contemporary copy should still sound like the family: plain and warm, not loud.
