# AGENTHON 2026: Track 2 pitch slide text

<!-- (2026-11-21) -->

Text to put on each slide of the 5-minute pitch, following the [pitch deck outline](track2-pitch.md), which has the timing, speakers and talking points.
A first version of the deck built from this text is in the [HTML export](slides/track2-pitch-deck.html ":ignore") under `docs/slides/`: open it in a browser to see every slide with its speaker notes.
To get a PDF with one slide per page and no speaker notes, run `scripts/deck-to-pdf.sh` (it needs Chromium or Chrome); every published GitHub release also gets this PDF attached by the [Pitch deck PDF workflow](https://github.com/B-AROL-O/agenthon-2026/blob/main/.github/workflows/deck-pdf.yml).

- **On-screen text is in English**, so every juror can read it; campaign copy stays in Italian, with the English underneath.
  The pitch itself is delivered in English, as agreed with the organizers (see the [pitch outline](track2-pitch.md)).
- **One idea per slide, few words.** Each slide lists the text, the visual, and what the speaker covers; the line in bold at the top of the text is the slide title, set large.
- **Fonts and colours from the campaign:** `Playfair Display` for titles, `Lato` for text; sky blue `#8DC6E8`, cream `#F9F2E9`, yellow `#F9C86A`.
  White text on sky blue, dark text on cream.
- Items in _italics with brackets_ are placeholders to fill in after production.

## Slide 1: Cold open

**Background:** black, then sky blue `#8DC6E8` after the live snap.

> **Si divide. Si condivide.**
>
> _It breaks. It's shared._

**Footer, small:** Team Macario · Track 2, GenAI for marketing · Feyles Rubatà

**Speaker:** live snap, pause, then «dividere» means two things: to break, and to share.

## Slide 2: The brief

**Visual:** the official packshots of `Il Classico` 200 g and `Il Cioccorubatà fondente` 150 g, the product of the Christmas spot, on cream.

> **The brief**
>
> Make the Rubatà the star of aperitivo at home, and a gift that stands out.
>
> **For** 25 to 40 year-olds
> **Deliver** a 20–30 s spot, social, web and print

**Speaker:** who Feyles is, what the brief asks, and the trap: explaining "sharing" with words, or a generic aperitivo where the product is a prop.

## Slide 3: The idea

**Visual:** split layout. Left half sky blue, right half cream; one snapped Rubatà across the split, each half on its own side.

> **«dividere»**
>
> **to break** · **to share**
>
> One gesture does both.

**Speaker:** snap one in half and you are already handing the other half to someone. We don't explain sharing: one breadstick becomes two. The stills show the snap; the Christmas spot tells the sharing, from 1968 to today. It uses the shape, the crunch and the act of sharing.

## Slide 4: The spot

**Visual:** the spot, full screen, 16:9, with sound. No text on top.

**Title card before playing, 1 second:**

> **30 seconds. A Christmas story, since 1968.**

**Caption after it ends:**

> Every shot, voice and sound in the spot made with AI.
> The pack and the logo are the real ones.

**Speaker:** nothing during the spot.

## Slide 5: Out in the world

**Visual:** the three mockups in `03-web-print/mockups/`, side by side on cream: the bus shelter in Turin, the billboard and the story on a phone.

> **From the street to the phone**

**Labels under the images, small:** Bus shelter, Turin · Billboard · Story on a phone

**Speaker:** ten seconds, right after the spot: this is where it lives, a bus shelter in Turin, a billboard, a story on your phone.

## Slide 6: The campaign

**Visual:** grid of six of the 80 static files on cream: `hero-ad-4x5`, `natale-ad-4x5`, `social-condivisione-9x16`, `social-finestra-1x1`, `range-completa-ad-4x5`, `print-poster-2x3`.
Names and descriptions of every file are in the [static visuals catalogue](track2-wp2-deliverables.md).
In the HTML deck the six images are in place, as web-sized copies of the Drive files in `docs/slides/img/`.

> **One gesture, every channel**
>
> 80 files · Ads · Social · Web · Print

**Labels under the images, small:**

- Hero ad: _«Spezzatelo. È fatto apposta.»_ (Break it. It's made for that.)
- Christmas ad: _«Dal 1968 si passa di mano.»_ (Handed down since 1968.)
- Social, condivisione: _«A chi va l'altra metà?»_ (Who gets the other half?)
- Social, finestra: _«Dentro c'è quello che deve esserci.»_ (Inside is what should be inside.)
- Full-range ad: _«Qual è il vostro?»_ (Which one is yours?)
- Poster: _«Si divide. | Si condivide.»_ (It breaks. It's shared.)

**Speaker:** one look everywhere: daylight, flat colours taken from the pack, layouts split in two like the snap. 80 files from two scripts, the year-round set and its Christmas edition with the Cioccorubatà, so a copy change rebuilds everything in under a minute.

## Slide 7: How we made it

**Visual:** a four-step pipeline, left to right, with an arrow between each step.

> **How we made it**
>
> **1. Real pack photos** → references for every image
> **2. AI images and clips** → _[image model]_, Google Veo with native sound
> **3. AI voice** → ElevenLabs, Italian voiceover
> **4. Post-production** → logo, claim and CTA added by hand, never generated

**Box at the bottom, yellow `#F9C86A`:**

> **Rules we gave the AI:** knobbly Rubatà only · no hands shaping dough · no generated text or logo · only facts from the client kit

**Speaker:** the pipeline, then one real problem and how it was solved: _[for example, a warped pack replaced with the real photo]_.

## Slide 8: Beyond the prototype

**Visual:** a row of the eight Rubatà cartons in their colours, each with a snapped breadstick in front; below, the friend-tag post.

> **A format, not a one-off**
>
> **8 flavours, 8 snaps:** same layout, same claim.
> **Built to spread:** _«A chi va l'altra metà?»_ invites people to post their own snap.
> **Proven to extend:** the Christmas edition: same pipeline, new season, new product.
> **Ready for the client:** written in the brand's own tone of voice.

**Speaker:** what we would do next for Feyles: one spot per flavour and the friend-tag challenge. The Christmas edition shows how cheap a new season is: same pipeline, new plates, new headlines.
