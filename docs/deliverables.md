## Deliverables in detail

### 1. Commercial

**The brief asks for** a 20 to 30 second video.

**We submit** one spot of about 26.5 seconds, exported in 16:9 and 9:16.
It opens on the snap of a Rubatà broken between two hands, goes back to the ear of wheat and the oven, then follows the breadstick under Turin's arcades to an aperitivo with friends, and closes on the claim.
Keyframes are Nano Banana 2 images referenced to the client's product photos, the clips are Veo 3.1 with native audio for the snaps, and the Italian voiceover is ElevenLabs.

**Where:** final exports in [`01-spot/`](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/13EHiNV1HQBJPxkDNWpmyLRpG7rdJucXo); the shot-by-shot beat sheet is in the [concept lock](track2-concept-lock.md).

### 2. Social media content set

**The brief asks for** a set of coordinated materials adaptable to the main social channels.

**We submit** five post concepts (`condivisione`, `finestra`, `torino`, `regalo`, `gamma`), each in the three formats the main channels use: 9:16 for stories, Reels and TikTok, 4:5 for the Instagram feed and 1:1 for the Facebook feed.
A six-slide 4:5 carousel retells the spot for viewers who never see the video.
Each file has its channel and caption in the copy deck.

**Where:** [`02-social/`](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1amFIgQr7ArG1DLYrSLLaNdptWTM_iiFC), one subfolder per concept; file list in the [static visuals catalogue](track2-wp2-deliverables.md), captions in the [social copy deck](track2-social-copy-deck.md).

### 3. Web and traditional advertising materials

**The brief asks for** visuals consistent with the campaign's creative concept.

**We submit** two parts.
For the web: two 16:9 banners, a desktop and mobile concept for the grissinificiofeyles.it home page hero, and four standard display ad sizes.
For print, each as a PNG and a print-ready PDF: a 2:3 poster, a 6×3 m billboard, a bus-shelter poster, an A4 magazine page and a gift tag.
Three mockups show the files in place, for the pitch and the project sheet.

**Where:** [`03-web-print/`](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/18gIPEFHZyNM86PuDFWHWoinOW7QiNQpd); file list in the [static visuals catalogue](track2-wp2-deliverables.md).

### 4. Advertising image

**The brief asks for** a coordinated visual, complete with headline and call to action (CTA).

**We submit** `hero-ad-4x5.png`: two hands of two different people snapping one Rubatà on sky blue, with the headline «Spezzatelo. È fatto apposta.» and the CTA «Scoprite gli otto gusti».
The same ad comes in 1:1 and 9:16, and a second ad, `range-ad-4x5.png`, shows the whole range.

**Where:** [`04-ad-image/`](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1Ot28S0gflXRGzg4BuRky1iISRpEJB-RL); file list in the [static visuals catalogue](track2-wp2-deliverables.md).

### 5. Project presentation sheet

**The brief asks for** a short description of the creative idea, the concept developed, the tools used and the role of artificial intelligence in the production process.

**We submit** a document built from the [project sheet](track2-project-sheet.md) draft: the double meaning of «dividere» (to break and to share), how the campaign answers the brief, the campaign system across formats, the rules we gave the AI to keep pack and product faithful, and how the campaign could run beyond the prototype.

**Where:** export the final version to [`05-project-sheet`](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1Vka12bMpa0K35WniqEP6akLKSiTJG9YL).

### 6. Tool details

**The brief asks for** a list of the platforms and software used, specifying how each was applied in the different production stages.

**We submit** a table built from the [tools list](track2-tools.md): generative AI, editing and design, and collaboration tools, each with the exact model or version, what it was used for and which deliverables it fed.

**Where:** export the final table to [`06-tools-list`](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1jHfqP7arUS2hm7aHMIjWepbRe3Z3Gw0f).
