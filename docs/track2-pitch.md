The deck is **not** a deliverable: keep it **outside** the submission Drive folder, so editing it after 17:00 does not touch the submitted files.

## Principles

- **The spot is the demo.** Play it full screen with sound, once, in the middle of the pitch. Test the audio in the room before starting.
- **One idea, said three times.** «Dividere»: to break and to share. Open with it, prove it with the spot, close with it.
- **Bring a real Rubatà.** Snap it live at the start and at the end: the jury hears the crunch the campaign is built on.
- **Few words on slides.** Big images from the campaign, one line of text each. The talking points are for the speaker, not the screen.
- **Cover the five criteria** explicitly: innovation, fit with the theme, feasibility beyond the prototype, technical execution, pitch quality.

## Slide by slide

### 1. Cold open (0:20)

- Snap a real Rubatà in front of the microphone. Pause.
- "In Italian, «dividere» means two things: to break, and to share."
- Show the claim: _«Si divide. Si condivide.»_

### 2. The brief (0:30)

- Feyles makes Rubatà, the knobbly rolled breadsticks of Piedmont, in Cambiano since 1968.
- The brief: make the grissino the star of aperitivo at home and a gift idea, for 25 to 40 year-olds, with a 20 to 30 second spot, social, web and print visuals.
- The trap: explaining "sharing" with words, or showing a generic aperitivo where the product is a prop.

### 3. The idea (0:40)

- A Rubatà does both meanings of «dividere» in one gesture: snap it in half, and you are already handing the other half to someone.
- So we do not explain sharing: we show the snap. One becomes two.
- It uses all three things the brief asked for: the shape, the crunch, the act of sharing.

### 4. Demo: the spot (0:40)

- One line before pressing play: "26 seconds. Listen to the first three."
- Play the spot full screen. Do not talk over it.
- After it ends, one line: "Every image, clip and voice is AI-generated. The pack and the logo are the real ones."

### 5. The campaign (0:40)

- One look across every channel: daylight, flat colours from the pack, layouts split in two halves like the snap.
- Walk the grid quickly: ad image _«Spezzatelo. È fatto apposta.»_, Instagram story, wheat-ear window macro, gift banner _«Portate qualcosa da dividere.»_, poster.

### 6. How we made it (0:50)

- Pipeline: official pack photos as references → AI keyframes and text-free plates → Veo clips with real snap sound → ElevenLabs Italian voiceover → logo, claim and CTA added in post. Name the exact tools from the [tools list](track2-tools.md).
- The rules we gave the AI: knobbly Rubatà only, never hands shaping dough, never generated text or logo, only facts from the client kit.
- One honest example of a problem and how we solved it (_to fill in after production_, for example a warped pack replaced by the real photo).

### 7. Beyond the prototype (0:50)

- **A format, not a one-off:** eight flavours, eight snaps, same layout and claim.
- **Built to spread:** _«A chi va l'altra metà?»_ invites people to post their own snap.
- **Cheap to extend:** text-free plates mean new headlines, seasons or gift boxes are a post-production change.
- **Ready for the client:** copy follows the brand's tone-of-voice guide and its approved CTAs.

### 8. Close (0:30)

- Snap a second Rubatà, hand half to a juror if appropriate.
- "One becomes two. That is the whole campaign."
- End card on screen: _«Si divide. Si condivide.»_ Thank the jury, invite questions.
