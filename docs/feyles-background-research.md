## Not to be confused with Grissini Derby

**Grissinificio Feyles** ([grissinificiofeyles.it](https://grissinificiofeyles.it/)) and **Grissini Derby** (`grissiniderby.it`) are two separate, unrelated companies that both make Rubatà in the Turin area.
Both invoke Piedmontese origins and the 17th-century Savoy court, so our visual references and copy never mix their packs, logos or product claims.

## Rubatà craft

Public sources describe what makes the product look and sound different on camera:

- **Rubatà versus stirato.**
  Both are listed among Piedmont's traditional agri-food products (PAT).
  _Stirato_ dough is pulled lengthwise to about 1.5 m and bakes thin, straight and even.
  _Rubatà_ ("rolled" in Piedmontese) is divided into rested portions (_filoncini_), rolled across a board dusted with fine bran (_cruschello_) into knobbly, irregular sticks of 40 to 50 cm, and baked in ovens whose temperature falls from about 250 °C to 200 °C until dry and crisp.
- **A festive bread.**
  Historical accounts quoted by Feyles describe the 17th- and 18th-century Rubatà as a festive delicacy for wealthy households, not as everyday bread.
- **Brand voice.**
  After a break-in at the Cambiano bakery in June 2025, Feyles posted on Instagram _«Lo capiamo: sono troppo buoni»_ ("We get it: they are too good"), a good example of its understated Piedmontese humour.

## Early narrative explorations and the pivot to the official brief

Before the official Track 2 brief was released at 10:28, our early research explored three Christmas-centred concepts for the commercial:

1. **17 km of aroma:** from the 7:00 oven in Cambiano to the evening lights under the Piazza Castello arcades in Turin, ending with a _crack_ on a shared board.
2. **The first gift opened before midnight:** a Feyles box brought to a holiday party gets opened five minutes after the doorbell rings, instead of waiting under the tree.
3. **Every knot is different:** slow-motion macro shots contrasting the irregular Rubatà with uniform industrial breadsticks.

When the brief and the client kit arrived, the team locked a sharper direction in the [Track 2 concept lock](track2-concept-lock.md):

- **No seasonal framing:** the brief does not mention Christmas. It targets 25 to 40 year-olds, with aperitivo at home and gifts to share all year round.
- **A narrower catalogue:** instead of showing all 19 products in 26 seconds, the spot and hero visuals focus on one gesture and one hero pack, `Il Classico` 200 g.
- **What we kept:** the crisp snap that opens the social moment, the irregular knobbly shape, the closeness to Turin's arcades, and the gift reframed as a gesture of sharing.
