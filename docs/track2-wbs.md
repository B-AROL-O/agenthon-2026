Plan for splitting the Track 2 workload on 21 November 2026.
The challenge itself is in the [Track 2 challenge brief](track2-challenge.md).
All times are local (Torino).

## Key constraints

- Submissions close at **17:00**. The submission is a Google Drive folder:
  at 17:00 the portal records when each file last changed, and anything changed after that does not count.
- The portal also asks for a project name and a description.
- Finalists pitch at **17:15**, only 15 minutes after the deadline, so the pitch must be ready before 17:00.
- About 4.5 hours of build time are left after 11:30, split by a one-hour lunch.

### Checkpoints

- **12:00**: the concept is frozen. Nobody reopens it.
- **15:45**: anything not done is cut. An extra social format is worth less than a finished spot.

## Work breakdown structure

### WP0: Concept and creative direction

Owner: all, until 12:00.

- Read the materials: logo, product photos, verified product info, tone of voice guide.
- Pick one concept built on the grissino's shape, its crunch or the act of sharing it,
  set in an aperitivo or gift moment for 25 to 40 year-olds.
- Fix the visual style, palette, headline and CTA.
- Product-fidelity rule: generate around the real product photos, never redraw the packaging.

### WP1: Advertising spot, 20 to 30 seconds

Owner: Luca. Deliverable 1.

1. Script and storyboard, 5 to 7 shots
2. Keyframe images, with the product photos as reference
3. Video clips, for example with Veo
4. Voiceover with ElevenLabs (also targets the "Best use of ElevenLabs" prize), music and sound effects
5. Edit and export in 16:9 and 9:16

### WP2: Static visuals

Owner: Alessio. Deliverables 2, 3 and 4.

1. Main advertising image with headline and CTA
2. Social media set: 1:1, 4:5 and 9:16, using frames from the spot plus 2 to 3 posts
3. Web banners and a print poster

### WP3: Documentation, submission and pitch

Owner: Gianpaolo. Deliverables 5 and 6, plus the submission and the pitch.

1. Create and share the Drive folder now, with this structure:
   `01-spot/`, `02-social/`, `03-web-print/`, `04-ad-image/`, `05-project-sheet`, `06-tools-list`
2. Fill in the project name and description on the portal early; they can be edited later
3. Project sheet: idea, concept, tools and the role of AI
4. Tools list, kept up to date during the day: platform or software, and what it was used for
5. Pitch deck, about 5 minutes, with the spot as the demo.
   Keep it **outside** the submission folder, so editing it after 17:00 does not touch the submitted files
6. Keep this repository up to date

### Final review

Owner: all, 16:15 to 16:30.
Check product fidelity, brand and tone of voice, spelling, and that all six deliverables are in the Drive folder.
